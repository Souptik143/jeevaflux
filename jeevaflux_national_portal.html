Risk Mitigation

Addressing Real-World Deployment Challenges

Proactive engineering strategies ensuring accuracy, battery life, and high accessibility across diverse demographics.

Environmental Robustness

Sensor Accuracy in Heat, Dust & Sweat

Multi-sensor fusion algorithms with outlier rejection and automated baseline calibration to prevent false environmental readings.

Power Optimization

Battery Life vs. Continuous AI

Adaptive inference scheduling, low-power MCU states, and quantized TinyML models delivering multi-day battery autonomy.

Alert Management

Preventing False-Alert Fatigue

Tiered alert logic (Vibration → App Notification → Caregiver Escalation) paired with personalized individual thresholds.

Accessibility

Low Digital Literacy & Elderly Users

Simplified large-icon interfaces, regional Indian language support, and assisted onboarding via ASHA/community health workers.

Data Governance

Privacy & Security Compliance

Strict adherence to India's DPDP Act with local edge processing and explicit consent-based sharing.

Disaster Resilience

Connectivity Failure During Cyclones/Floods

Store-and-forward architecture with local AI inference continuing uninterrupted during total network blackouts.
