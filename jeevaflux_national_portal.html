Robust Offline-First Resilience (Zero-Internet Operation)

Jeevaflux is engineered for rural, disaster-relief, and remote deployment scenarios. Core health monitoring, anomaly detection, risk computation, and local alert generation operate entirely offline on the edge device. When internet connectivity is restored, encrypted telemetry packets synchronize automatically with the Ministry's national server nodes.

Privacy, Data Minimization & Security Protocols

Compliant with National Digital Health Mission (NDHM) and stringent government data protection standards

Local & On-Device Processing

All biometric calculations and environmental risk fusions are computed locally on the wearable device or gateway smartphone. Raw physiological streams never leave your personal hardware without explicit consent.

Encrypted Local Storage

Stored telemetry logs are secured using military-grade AES-256 local encryption keys. Data at rest cannot be accessed by third-party applications or unauthorized agents.

Strict Data Minimization

Jeevaflux collects only essential public health telemetry required for municipal monitoring. Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is decoupled from telemetry streams through randomized cryptographic hashes.

Secure Optional Synchronization

Cloud transmission to national public health servers is strictly optional and executed over TLS 1.3 encrypted tunnels authenticated via secure government gateway tokens.

National Health Research Telemetry Contribution

Contribute anonymized aggregate environmental-health correlation data to the Ministry's public health research repository.
