# Future Scope

## 1. Advanced Personalization

- Adaptive user-specific risk models
- Longitudinal baselines
- Uncertainty-aware predictions

## 2. Expanded Sensing

Potential future additions include validated physiological, environmental and
motion sensors where they improve risk detection.

## 3. Predictive Health Intelligence

Move from detecting current abnormal patterns toward predicting emerging risk
with sufficient validation evidence.

## 4. Disaster Intelligence

Extend environmental-context support for:

- Heat waves
- Pollution events
- Flood-related conditions
- Other India-relevant environmental/disaster scenarios

## 5. Privacy-Preserving Learning

Explore federated or privacy-preserving learning after the local system is
validated and an appropriate data-governance model exists.

## 6. Accessibility

- Regional languages
- Voice assistance
- Elderly-friendly interfaces
- Caregiver workflows with explicit consent

## 7. Research

- Longitudinal field studies
- Sensor-fusion comparisons
- Fairness and generalization studies
- Edge-AI optimization
- Privacy-preserving datasets

## 8. Commercialization

Potential directions include consumer hardware, institutional programs and
software/support offerings, subject to validation and regulatory requirements.

## 9. Long-Term Impact

The long-term objective is a resilient health-support ecosystem that can provide
useful early warnings even when internet connectivity is unreliable.
