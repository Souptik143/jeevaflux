## Short Description

and environmental sensing with personalized risk assessment to provide early
warnings while preserving privacy and supporting offline operation.

## 50-Word Summary

JeevaFlux is a privacy-preserving health companion designed for continuous
monitoring and early warning. It combines physiological signals, environmental
conditions and personal context using lightweight on-device intelligence. Its
offline-first architecture aims to keep core monitoring useful during
connectivity failures, making it relevant to heat, pollution and disaster
scenarios.

## 1-Minute Pitch

Health risk is not determined by a single vital sign. It can emerge from the
interaction of physiology, activity and environmental exposure. JeevaFlux brings
these signals together using edge intelligence, personalized baselines and
sensor fusion. The result is an early-warning companion designed to remain
useful even when connectivity is unreliable, while minimizing unnecessary
transfer of sensitive health data.
