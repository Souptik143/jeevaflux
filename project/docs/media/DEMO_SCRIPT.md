# JeevaFlux Demo Script

## 60–90 Second Demo

### Scene 1 — The Problem
Show the wearable and explain:
"JeevaFlux is designed to help people recognize health risks earlier, especially
when heat, pollution or disaster conditions make the environment dangerous."

### Scene 2 — Sensing
Show the device:
"Physiological and environmental signals are collected continuously."

### Scene 3 — Edge AI
Show the architecture:
"Instead of sending everything to the cloud, JeevaFlux performs key processing
and risk inference locally."

### Scene 4 — Risk Detection
Show the mobile dashboard:
"The system combines signals and personal context to generate a graded early
warning."

### Scene 5 — Offline Resilience
Disable connectivity:
"Core monitoring is designed to continue locally, with synchronization after
connectivity returns."

### Scene 6 — Privacy
Show the privacy architecture:
"Sensitive health processing is designed around data minimization and local
intelligence."

### Closing
"JeevaFlux — continuous, contextual and privacy-preserving health support."
